# Downloads

The lecture notes in PDF, with the same numbering of definitions, theorems, examples and exercises as the website. Text and figures are released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en).

## Lecture notes 1 · Models, bounds and formulations

What an integer linear programming problem is (MIP, ILP, BIP), its solutions and outcomes; primal and dual bounds and the gap; the LP relaxation; ideal formulations, valid inequalities and cover inequalities. Worked examples and exercises with complete solutions.
